About Us

The Turing Scholars program, named after the mathematician Alan Turing, is an honors program for computer science majors at The University of Texas at Austin. The program, launched in the fall of 2002, admits no more than 50 students each year.

At a Glance

The Turing Scholars Program fosters a close-knit community through small honors classes, faculty mentorship, undergraduate research, and student-organized events. Students complete a rigorous honors thesis while gaining valuable research and communication skills. Beyond academics, Turing Scholars benefit from exceptional career opportunities, including competitive internships, strong industry connections, and placement in top PhD programs.

Intramural Sports

Turing Scholars enjoy a vibrant social community through intramural sports, weekly events, and shared study spaces. From soccer and Ultimate Frisbee teams to collaborative study sessions in the Honors Quad and Gates Dell Complex, students have countless opportunities to connect, build friendships, and support one another beyond the classroom.

Student Spread

The Turing Scholars program brings together a diverse community of students from across the United States and around the world. Each year, our incoming class includes students from a wide range of geographic, cultural, and educational backgrounds, including several international students. Despite different backgrounds, students share a common curiosity and passion for computer science.
