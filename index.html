About Us

The Turing Scholars Student Association (TSSA) is a student-led organization dedicated to building a welcoming and connected community within UT Austin’s Turing Scholars program. Through social events, professional development opportunities, and student-led initiatives, we bring together students who share a passion for computer science. Our goal is to foster meaningful friendships, encourage collaboration, and help every Turing Scholar make the most of their college experience.

Events

Throughout the year, TSSA organizes a wide range of events to help Turing Scholars connect with one another outside of the classroom. From social events and fun activities to company-sponsored events, there's always something going on! The goal is to create an environment to meet people across different class years, get to know the Turing community, and take a break from the usual coursework.
